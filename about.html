Sydney, Australia

I’m Danny, and I’m a storyteller. I’ve been exploring art as a way to tell stories since I could hold a crayon.

Such hits of mine lost to the wilderness of time include a picture-book retelling of Robin Hood, a shot-for-shot comic book adaptation of Star Wars, and a Final Fantasy VIII fan fiction. Sure, maybe not the most original start, but now I’m building grand stories and adventures.

If drawing was my first love, writing is my true love.

I’ll always have a soft spot for a sketch, but the architecture of books is where I belong.

I studied Creative Writing at Deakin and have worked as a copywriter, technical writer and screenwriter, all in service of my purer love: the novel.

These days I’m writing fantasy for adults young and old. See what I’m working on over on the Books page.

Oh, yeah, the non-writer bits… I live in Sydney with my wife, our son and two cats. So, yes, I’m at the bottom of the food chain here.
